# Mohamed Tersawy

Seven years building software end to end — the data model, the API, the interface, and the
infrastructure all of it runs on. I'm most useful where those are one person's problem rather
than four teams' handoffs.

Right now that means **Golify**, a multi-tenant SaaS ERP for logistics and accounting. I wrote the
company's original in-house system; when the market turned out to be bigger than one company, I
led its re-platform into a commercial product, and have owned it since.

Before that: penetration-testing labs at Cyber Sky, where every button in the UI became a
Terraform plan and a cloud bill. Two years freelancing across inventory, CRM, auctions and
education. And a hospital ERP in 2019 that taught me what a bad schema costs two years later.

**[tersawy.com](https://tersawy.com)** has the long version — a database per tenant resolved at
the edge, a double-entry ledger under the whole product, and what it takes to change either of
them while the system stays up and paying customers keep using it.

Most of my work lives in a private company monorepo, so this profile is a lot quieter than my
week is. What's here is the overflow.
